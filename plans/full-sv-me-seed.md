## Coverage gaps (intentional, documented)

1. **SV1 has no SNKRDUNK rows.** Probing `pkmn-tcg-sv1-001` returns an
   empty apparel list, while `pkmn-tcg-sv1v-001` works. SNKRDUNK lumps
   the Scarlet+Violet base cards under the `SV1V` product-number
   namespace. This is a source-side taxonomy choice we can't override
   without dual-indexing the same cards. Cardrush alone covers SV1
   (692 rows).

2. **M1 (Mega Brave) has no SNKRDUNK rows.** `pkmn-tcg-m1-001` is empty
   but `pkmn-tcg-m1s-001` works. Likely a recency issue (M1 hasn't
   been indexed there yet). Cardrush covers M1 (422 rows).

3. **243 artworks still lack `name_ja`.** Distribution:
   - SV4A: 88 (many Shiny Treasure reprints have no Cardrush listing)
   - M2A: 128 (Commons — known from the previous session)
   - Scattered across early SV sets: ~27
   These will fill in naturally as Cardrush starts listing those cards;
   `make catalog-bootstrap SET=<code>` is rerunnable and idempotent
   against unmarked YMLs.

4. **asyncpg 32767-arg limit** tripped on 1 card in each of SV8A and
   M2A during SNKRDUNK (`attempted=291 succeeded=290`,
   `attempted=250 succeeded=249`). Same gotcha as the previous session.
   Low-priority fix: chunk `insert_price_points` at, say, 1000 rows.

## How to reproduce / extend

To add a 29th set later:

1. Create `data/sets/<CODE>.yml` with `set_code`, `era_block`,
   `language`, `name_ja`, `name_en` (optional), `tcgdex_id` (optional),
   and `pokellector_slug`.
2. `make catalog-seed-sets`
3. `make catalog-bootstrap SET=<CODE>`
4. `make catalog-seed-cards SET=<CODE>`
5. `make catalog-verify`
6. `make scrape-cardrush SET=<CODE>`
7. `make scrape-snkrdunk ERA=<code>`  (lowercase; passes through to
   `resolve_apparel` as the `{era}` component of `pkmn-tcg-{era}-{NNN}`)

The post-scrape hook refreshes the read-side MVs automatically.
